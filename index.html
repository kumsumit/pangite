09A little more clarity

Good questions. Straightforward answers.

Get to know the idea, the possibilities and the details that matter.

What is Pangeabuy?

Pangeabuy is a hyper-commerce platform developed by Kumpali Private Limited, designed to help people discover relevant products and participating merchants in their local market.

What does hyper-commerce mean?

Here, it means a shopping and discovery experience focused on relevant products and participating merchants in a local market. The purchase options depend on the capabilities actually supported.

Can I find products from nearby shops?

That is the intended experience. Actual options will depend on participating merchants, available listings and local coverage. This introductory website does not provide live product search or location-based results.

Can I buy products through Pangeabuy?

This website is an introduction only. Purchases will be made in the Pangeabuy desktop and mobile apps, and the purchase journey will depend on the options supported for the relevant listing and merchant.

Does Pangeabuy offer home delivery?

Delivery is not handled on this website; it will be part of the apps. Delivery, pickup and other fulfilment options depend on the merchant and the capabilities actually implemented for a listing.

How can a merchant join Pangeabuy?

Merchant onboarding and registration will take place in the Pangeabuy desktop and mobile apps. Pangeabuy is scheduled to launch on 14 December 2026. Updated joining information will be added when available.

How are Pangeabuy and Stromule connected?

Pangeabuy focuses on customer-facing discovery and commerce. Stromule focuses on business organisation. Where an integration is implemented and authorised, eligible merchant-maintained information can support Pangeabuy discovery. Private business records do not automatically become public.

Are all products and merchants available everywhere?

No. Pangeabuy launches on 14 December 2026, and coverage will grow with merchant participation. Availability depends on actual merchant participation, location coverage and the information on the platform. Illustrative examples on this website do not represent live inventory or confirmed launch categories.

How does Pangeabuy use product information?

Relevant merchant-maintained information is intended to support customer-facing discovery where the implementation, permissions and product rules allow it. This site has no connected merchant data or product feed. Platform-specific policies must be published before a live service is introduced.
